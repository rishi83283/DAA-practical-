In this practical, we implemented Prim's Algorithm to determine the Minimum Spanning Tree (MST) of a weighted connected undirected graph:

1. Algorithm Details:
   - Greedy Strategy: Starts at an arbitrary root vertex and grows the tree one edge at a time.
   - At each step, picks the cheapest edge connecting a vertex in the MST to a vertex outside the MST using a min-heap priority queue.

2. Performance Metrics:
   - Time Complexity: O((V + E) log V), where V is the number of vertices and E is the number of edges.
   - Space Complexity: O(V + E) for storing adjacency lists and priority queue elements.

note:
- Prim's algorithm is particularly suitable and faster for dense graphs where the number of edges E is close to V^2.
